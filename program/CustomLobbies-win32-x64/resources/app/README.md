## 🌟 Feature Overview

### 🌿 1. Modular Botany & Cannabis Cultivation
* **4 Distinct Strains**: OG Kush (Indica), Purple Haze (Sativa), Gelato #41 (Hybrid), Blue Dream (High-THC).
* **Soil & Nutrients**: Coco Coir / Perlite substrate, moisture regulation, and NPK nutrient balancing (Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Potassium).
* **GMod-Style Physical Planter Melding**:
  * Manually snap Quantum Board LED fixtures, cooling radiators, 50L nutrient water tanks, and dual planting pots onto base frames.
  * 35cm magnetic proximity snap with green holographic ghost alignment previews & rigid physical welds.
* **Genetics, Cloning & Curing**:
  * Take genetic clone cuttings from mother plants with cloning shears.
  * Cross-breed strains to create custom high-THC Pacifica hybrids.
  * Glass jar terpene aging (*Fresh $\rightarrow$ Rack Dried $\rightarrow$ Silver $\rightarrow$ Gold $\rightarrow$ Diamond 35%+ THC*).

* **Property Tiers**:
  * *Skidrow Motel Room #104* (Low-rent starter spot).
  * *Vinewood Suburb Stash House* (Basement grow room, high power limit).
  * *Skyline Heights Penthouse 42B* (Downtown luxury, maximum security).

* **Stealth In-House Fixtures**:
  * **Mylar Reflective Grow Tents**: +25% yield and blocks 80% of infrared heat.
  * **Inline Carbon Scrubbers**: Eliminates 88% of terpenes and odors.
  * **Lab Fume Extraction Hood**: Traps 95% of toxic chemical gases.
  * **50-Amp Dual Power Sub-Panels**: Adds +25 kW power capacity.
  * **Concealed False-Wall Safes**: 24-slot hidden contraband storage.

## 📦 Installation & Dedicated Server Deployment

### For Steam HELIX Dedicated Server:
1. Double-click `Launch_Steam_Dedicated_Server.bat` in this repository.
2. In the HELIX game client, open console (`~`) and type:
   ```text
   open 127.0.0.1:7777
   ```

### For Singleplayer / Direct Client Play:
1. In the HELIX game menu, press **`~`** and type:
   ```text
   open /Game/Pacifica/Map/Pacifica?Experience=BP_Experience_Sandbox
   ```
