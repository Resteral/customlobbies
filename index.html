📖 Field Operator's Manual

CustomLobbies is your central command for high-stakes matchmaking, competitive drafting, and private scrims. Master the platform ecosystem with the steps below.

1. Setting Up: Teams vs Solos

🛡️

Before joining a match, you need to decide how you're deploying:

👥 Making a Team: Navigate to the Teams tab and click "Register Team". You will become the Captain. From there, you can invite players from the Community Hub or post a recruitment ad to build your roster.

🐕 Playing Solo: Head to the Teams tab and register as a Solo Mercenary to get picked up by Teams, or simply queue directly into Lobbies as a free agent.

2. Joining Lobbies

🏟️

Once your squad is formed (or if you're flying solo), head over to the Lobbies & Queue tab.

For Teams: The Team Captain selects a lobby and clicks "Join Lobby". This will automatically pull the entire active roster into the lobby waiting room with them.

For Solos: Browse the active lobbies and click "Join" to fill empty slots, or use the Quick Queue system to be automatically placed into a balanced match based on your MMR.

3. The Gatekeeper Check

🎤

We maintain a high standard for competitive play. When you or your team apply to join a premium verified lobby, you may be prompted by the Gatekeeper System.

This requires you to record a quick 5-second video/mic introduction. The lobby host reviews this "vibe check" to ensure your microphone works and you aren't toxic before approving your entry into the lobby.

4. Joining The Match

🚀

When the lobby is full, a 15-second Ready Check appears. Once all players accept, the Captains perform a map veto.

After the veto, the system generates a private match server. CustomLobbies automatically copies the Direct Connect code to your clipboard and will prompt your game (like CS2) to launch directly into the server via Steam. No manual IP typing required—just click accept, and you're in!
